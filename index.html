How the projections work

  • Election-day votes at each venue = ordinary votes projected by the early vote tracker for every voting location there (host district and any joint districts), plus absent and other election-day votes. Each district's election-day vote is its expected turnout less revised early and postal votes, so the figures move each morning with the VEC's early vote report.
  • Absent votes: the statewide pool is spread over venues in proportion to the VEC's absent vote estimates. Who casts them comes from a gravity model on distance to each district (voters vote absent close to home). This is how a venue outside the two regions, such as in the CBD or Bundoora, picks up Northern or Western Metro voters.
  • Northern + Western Metro upper house votes = ordinary votes from districts in those regions plus their absent voters at the venue. Both upper house votes count wherever they are cast; other regions' votes are left out.
  • Potential VS vote = those upper house votes x the best past VS share at the venue: 2022 state lower house, 2022 upper house, 2025 Senate, or 2025 House where VS ran (the same figures as the voting-centres pages on the map sites). Absent voters use their home district's average.
  • Projected support: Full multiplies each part by the doorknock-model support index of the voters there (100 = Northern + Western Metro average; Northern Metro voters are about 9% more supportive than Western Metro voters on average). Half multiplies by its square root, so an index of 121 adds 10% instead of 21%. Past VS share and doorknock support overlap (they correlate about 0.5 across centres), so Full partly counts the same support twice; Half keeps the extra information without that. Venues outside the two regions use the home districts of their Northern and Western Metro voters.
  • District rank is among venues hosted by that district; region rank among venues in the host's upper house region. A district view also lists venues of other districts where its voters vote ordinary (joint centres).
  • Locations: map site venue files, else 2022 booth coordinates. venues outside the two regions have no exact location and are left off the map (they stay in the table).
