How the projections work

  • NM + WM upper house votes: projected election-day votes at the centre from voters in Northern Metro and Western Metro districts, including absent voters. Both upper house votes count wherever they are cast; other regions' votes are left out. Election-day votes for each district = expected turnout less early and postal votes, from the early vote tracker, so the figures move each morning with the VEC's early vote report. Absent votes are placed with a distance model (voters vote absent close to home).
  • Potential VS vote: It also takes in our doorknock model at half strength (a centre whose voters are 21% more supportive than average gets 10% more).
  • Warm Labor voters: of the upper house voters there, Labor voters (2025 House first preferences at nearby polling places) who put VS in their top 3 on the 2025 Senate ballot. The voters most open to giving us an upper house vote.
  • Door pool: how many of the upper house voters there our doorknock model rates as likely VS supporters (support, discounted for opposition, from everyone on the roll near the centre).
  • District rank is among centres hosted by that district; region rank among centres in the host's upper house region. A district view also lists centres in other districts where its voters vote (joint centres).
  • Map locations: map site venue files, else 2022 booth coordinates. centres outside the two regions have no exact location and are left off the map (they stay in the table).
